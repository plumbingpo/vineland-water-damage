Water Heater Leak Cleanup Help in Vineland

A water heater holds 40 to 80 gallons, but that is not the limit of the leak. Because the supply line feeds the tank, a rusted out bottom drains and refills at the same pace. Until someone closes the cold water valve, it keeps running. Where the unit sits decides the damage: a garage floor, a hallway closet or, worst of all, an attic above finished rooms.

After extracting the water, our Vineland crew checks the materials that sat next to the unit. The platform beneath the tank and the base of the closet walls are usually soaked. When the heater is in the garage, items kept on the slab get wet and so does the wall adjoining the house. Attic leaks travel downward through the insulation and the ceilings below. We measure each area and dry it with air movers and dehumidifiers.

Do This First

What to Do Right Now

  1. Turn Off the Heat SourceFor an electric heater, switch off its breaker. On a gas unit, rotate the gas control to its off setting. A burner or heating element must not stay on once the tank is empty.
  2. Shut the Cold Water ValveFind the cold water shutoff above the heater and turn it clockwise. A stuck or leaking valve means going to the house main and shutting the water off there.
  3. Watch for Hot WaterWater from the tank can scald. Let pooled water cool off before anyone steps in it, and keep kids and pets out of the area.
  4. Empty the Tank OutsideWhen the drain valve is within reach, connect a garden hose and lead it to a floor drain or outdoors, which keeps the rest of the water off the floor.
  5. Move Stored ItemsLift boxes, rugs and anything absorbent off the wet floor. Fabric and cardboard absorb water fast, then keep it pressed to the concrete and walls.

Why It Happens

How It Usually Starts

  • The drain valve at the bottom of the tank cracking, loosening or failing to shut fully.
  • Sediment and corrosion that eat through the bottom of an aging steel tank.
  • Excess pressure in the tank from a failed thermostat or a missing expansion tank.
  • A blocked or rusted drain pan under an attic or closet heater that overflows instead of draining.
  • A temperature and pressure relief valve that opens or fails and discharges water.
If It Is Left

What Happens if You Wait

Platform and Subfloor

Under the tank, the wood stand or the subfloor absorbs the largest share of the leak. That wood may rot and lose strength, and it will have to carry the weight of a new tank filled with water.

Items Stored Near the Heater

Whatever is kept by a closet or garage heater, from keepsakes to furniture and boxes, is the first thing to get wet. Mold can develop on damp contents that are not promptly dried or tossed, while cardboard boxes disintegrate.

Garage Wall Base

The wall between the garage and the house takes water into its bottom plate and drywall from the slab, and from there it reaches the floors of the living space.

Ceilings Under an Attic Heater

When an attic heater leaks, water passes through the insulation and lands on the ceiling underneath. Wet insulation stops insulating and its weight can bring ceiling drywall down.

Will Insurance Pay?

Does Insurance Cover It?

A sudden tank failure is often treated as accidental and sudden, so the resulting damage to belongings, floors and walls is often covered. Replacing the heater is usually not included, because a tank wearing out from age counts as maintenance. If an adjuster finds signs of a long-term leak, such as rust trails or old staining, the claim can be reduced or denied because gradual leaks usually are not covered. Check your policy, photograph the heater and the damage, and keep the old unit until the claim is settled.

Cost

How the Quote Is Built

  • Placement of the unit, as leaks from an attic or an inside closet run higher than those in a garage.
  • Whether flooring in adjoining rooms was reached and what type it is.
  • Whether the platform or subfloor under the tank has to be rebuilt before a new heater goes in.
  • The amount of wall drywall, ceiling drywall or insulation that has to be removed.
