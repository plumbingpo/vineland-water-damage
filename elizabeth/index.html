Where Water Damage Starts in Elizabeth Properties

Some water problems need a phone call this minute, and others can safely wait until morning. The five below are ones we see constantly in Elizabeth, listed from most pressing to least.

Bursts: A pipe that has split is the most urgent call there is, because it puts out water for as long as the supply is on. Cold weather, depending on the season, is one trigger, with the leak appearing as the frozen section thaws. Shut the house supply, run a tap to drain the lines and phone us immediately, whatever the hour. Ceilings that bulge with trapped water should be left alone and the room below kept empty.

Runoff: Storm water coming under a door or through a garage is next in line. You cannot stop the rain, but you can move belongings up and keep away from any outlets near the water. Having passed over pavement and soil on the way in, it carries dirt and germs. As soon as it is safe to enter, we pump the water out and take away porous items it ruined.

Kitchens: A dishwasher that leaks from its door or a refrigerator line that drips behind the unit calls for same-day attention. Turning off the supply valve for that appliance normally ends the leak itself. The harm continues, though, since water has already run under cabinets and flooring where air does not move. For Elizabeth kitchen jobs, our crew can frequently save the cabinets by pushing dry air in at the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Moisture under a raised floor is seldom an emergency, but it should not be put off for a season. Puddles on the ground cover, dripping ductwork or wet insulation between the joists are what an inspection usually turns up. Given time, that humidity works its way upstairs and the joists start to rot. Our crew clears any standing water, brings the framing down to a normal moisture level and reports the likely cause.

Odors: A musty smell near the site of an old leak is the least urgent item here, yet it deserves an inspection soon. Mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a material getting wet, so a leak from months ago had plenty of time. Avoid wiping or bleaching a big area on your own, as that sends spores into other rooms. Our technicians test the area, contain it and remove the growth along with the moisture feeding it across Elizabeth.

Preventing Water Damage in Elizabeth Homes

Houses tell you when water is getting where it should not. Our technicians see the same early clues again and again in Elizabeth, and they are worth learning. Blistering paint, ceiling stains, warped baseboards and floorboards that curl at the edges each point to water behind or beneath them. A musty smell in a closet, a toilet that rocks, or a tap that drips belongs on the list too.

Taken singly they are minor, though every one of them gets more expensive with time. Re-caulk tubs and showers when the bead cracks, since water behind tile does damage you will not see for months. Test a sump pump several times a year by filling the pit until the float trips and the motor runs. In basements and garages, shelving and plastic tubs keep belongings clear of the floor.

No amount of maintenance prevents every leak. What it does is make them rarer and smaller, and that matters when the alternative is days of drying equipment in your Elizabeth living room.
