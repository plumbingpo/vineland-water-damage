Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Learn where the main water shutoff is and how to close it if pipes are damaged in a storm
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often

Signs You Need Storm Damage Repair in Vineland

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Flooding in lower levels because drainage systems were overwhelmed
  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm

Why Storm Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

Storm damage creates complex, connected repair needs that take experienced restoration professionals to handle properly. Wet insulation, soaked roof decking and damp wall cavities often sit behind a leak that appears small, and without proper detection and drying they bring mold and a weakening structure. Our technicians trace where the water traveled using thermal imaging plus moisture meters, so no affected area is overlooked.

Emergency board-up and tarping also take proper technique to be effective. If a tarp is fastened poorly, it can direct water where you do not want it or give out when the wind picks up.
