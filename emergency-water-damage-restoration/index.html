Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Trapped water overhead has made part of the ceiling bulge or sag.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • The tank on your water heater gave out and its contents are spreading through the room.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
